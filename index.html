About us

At Bhansa, food is more than a meal—it's a way of bringing people together. We proudly serve authentic vegetarian Indian, Chinese, and Tandoor specialties, prepared with fresh ingredients and genuine care. Whether you're joining us for a family dinner, a casual outing, or a special celebration, every visit is designed to feel warm, welcoming, and memorable.

Authors of Authentic Taste.

We believe great food brings people closer. That's why we focus on authentic flavors, fresh ingredients, and warm hospitality—creating a place where every meal becomes a memorable experience.
